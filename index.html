About

Twelve years of looking closely.

I started out assisting fashion photographers in London before trading the studio for natural light and real places. Today I split my time between editorial commissions, brand storytelling and a handful of weddings each year.

My approach is unhurried. I spend time with people before I photograph them, and I'd rather wait for the right light than add more of it.
